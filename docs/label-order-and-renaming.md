# Arrange and rename labels

In the expanded mail sidebar, drag the small grip beside a label. On a phone, hold the grip or label row briefly, then move it. A preview and blue insertion line show the destination; releasing saves the new order. Long lists scroll automatically near their top and bottom edges. Escape or cancelling the touch gesture leaves the order unchanged. The grip also accepts the up/down arrow keys.

Reordering stays within the current sibling group. Moving a parent carries its children with it; nesting is changed separately in label settings. Order is saved per mailbox and sibling group in the current browser, survives reloads and updates other tabs of that browser. It is a local display preference, so another device can use its own order. If local storage is unavailable, the order lasts for the current session.

Open a label's three-dot menu and choose **Rename**. Saving updates the existing label, preserving its ID, children, assigned messages and rule targets. Sidebar paths, message label chips and label pickers refresh after saving. Names must be nonempty, at most 80 characters and unique within the mailbox. Renaming requires mailbox management access; read-only users can still rearrange their local sidebar.
